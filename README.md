# AI for Pharmaceutical Sales & Cross-Functional Excellence — David Hooi

English landing page for D Action Consultancy's 2-day programme, built in the DACS landing page template.
Day 1 — Prompting, Content, Research & Your AI Assistant · Day 2 — Dashboards, Visuals & AI Roadmap.

Intended live URL: https://dhooi266.github.io/ai-pharma-cross-functional/

- Source parts (`_body.html`, `_base.css`, `_extra.css`) are assembled into `index.html` by `node _build.js`
- `node _render.js` renders the 1200×630 share card `og-share-v1.jpg` from `og-card-source.html`
- Hero image `assets/course-hero.jpg` is a placeholder reused from ai-corporate-training
- Local preview: `node .serve.js` → http://localhost:3033
