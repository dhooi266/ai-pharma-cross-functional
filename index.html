About the programme

One programme for the whole pharma team, not just sales

AI for Pharmaceutical Sales & Cross-Functional Excellence builds practical AI skills across the functions that make a pharma business run. Day 1 covers prompting, the leading AI models, AI for slides and research, your own AI assistant, and data and financial analysis. Prompt chaining in action: from CRM data to a personalised HCP plan

Each step feeds the next. This is the HCP profiling workflow participants practise with mock CRM data.

CRM dataExport anonymised HCP records: specialty, region, visit history.
SegmentsAI groups HCPs by specialty, goals and engagement level.
PersonasEach segment becomes a persona with needs, concerns and preferred channels.
Engagement planTailored messages, channels and visit frequency for each persona.
Next visitThe rep reviews the plan and personalises the next HCP call.
AI drafts, people decide. Participants learn to mask data before uploading, never enter patient or identifiable HCP data into public AI tools, check every clinical figure against its source (AI can invent studies), and send promotional and medical content through your company's approval process.
